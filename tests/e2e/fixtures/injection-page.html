Markets Rally as Energy Sector Leads Gains

Analysts reported a broad rally on Monday as energy stocks posted the strongest quarterly performance in over a year. Investors pointed to stable fuel demand and disciplined production quotas as the primary drivers behind the surge.

Meanwhile, retail spending held steady according to the latest employment snapshot, suggesting that household balance sheets remain resilient despite higher borrowing costs. Economists will publish a fuller revision next Friday.
